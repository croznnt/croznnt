### 🏭 About me

- 🎓 I **teach and research Industrial Management** — operations, production planning, quality and performance measurement.
- 📊 I build small, hands-on tools that help students *see* how factory data becomes **KPIs** (OEE, FPY, MTBF/MTTR, OTD…).
- 🧩 I like turning abstract concepts into **interactive dashboards and sample datasets** for the classroom.
- 🌱 Currently exploring **data visualization, forecasting and digital manufacturing (MES / Industry 4.0)**.
- 💬 Ask me about **OEE, Lean, Six Sigma, capacity planning, or building teaching demos with Python**.
